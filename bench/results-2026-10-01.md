# Benchmark results, 2026-10-01

`uv run clipper eval bench/videos.txt`, 27 videos listed, 25 graded (2 skipped on HTTP 403). Ranker at commit 510ad21 weights, Whisper large-v3 batched, `jev-latest`. Jev cost for the run: $0.46 (11.1M tokens).

## Result

The ranker does not predict most-replayed moments.

- Top-5 replay lift: mean 0.93x, median 0.93x, above 1.0 in 10 of 25 videos.
- Picks landing on a top-10% replay segment: 26%, against 30% for any candidate.

## Reading

- Replay is a doubtful label for "good clip". Moments that need earlier context are replayed more, which fits people rewinding because they missed something, not because the moment is great.
- Loudness is negatively related to replay in 19 of 25 videos. Its positive weight in `rank.py` has no support here.
- The questions that matter most to the weighted score (hook, payoff) show no relationship with replay in either direction.

## What this does not show

It does not show the picks are bad clips. Replay measures rewatching inside a long video; the target is views on a standalone short. Those can differ, and only posted-clip view counts can settle it.
